Metrics Manual

Our analysis engine combines powerful classical engines with custom algorithms to break down your game into understandable concepts. Here is what every metric means.

Positional Strategy

King Safety

Evaluates how vulnerable the king is to checks and mating attacks based on pawn cover and enemy piece proximity.

Pawn Structure

Analyzes the solidity of your pawn chain, penalizing doubled, isolated, or backward pawns.

Center Control

Measures your dominance over the four critical central squares (e4, d4, e5, d5) and adjacent territory.

Piece Coordination

Activity

Quantifies how "active" your pieces are, rewarding pieces placed on forward outposts or open lines.

Harmony

How well your pieces defend each other. High harmony means your army is a cohesive unit; low harmony means loose pieces.

Mobility

The raw number of legal moves available to you. More options generally indicate a more flexible position.

Tactical Sharpness

Material

The raw point value of the pieces on the board (Queen=9, Rook=5, etc.), adjusted for tactical imbalances.

Threats

Measures the intensity of immediate tactical dangers, such as hanging pieces or mate threats.

Aggression

How much forward pressure you are exerting. High aggression often leads to tactical complications.

Territory & Structure

Space

The amount of territory you control behind your pawn chains, giving your pieces room to maneuver.

Squeeze

Your ability to restrict the opponent's movement. A high squeeze score usually means the opponent is in "zugzwang".

Integrity

A measure of structural health. High integrity means no holes or weak squares in your camp.

Weakness

Targetable flaws in the position, such as backward pawns or weak color complexes.

Time & Decision Making

Criticality

How "sharp" the position is. In high-criticality positions, there is only one good move, and any mistake leads to immediate loss. Low criticality implies a forgiving position.

Complexity

An estimate of how difficult the position is for a human to calculate, based on the number of branching variations.

Time Efficiency

We analyze your clock usage versus the position's criticality to determine if you are "Thinking Deeply", "Rushing", or in "Time Trouble".

Move Quality

Direct comparison of your move against the best possible line calculated by the engine, categorized from "Blunder" to "Brilliant".
